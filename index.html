VeritasFlow

AI-Powered Digital Wellbeing

VeritasFlow is an AI-powered digital wellbeing platform that helps users understand and improve their online consumption habits. Through a browser extension and web dashboard, it tracks user-consented activity across platforms like YouTube, Reddit, X (Twitter), Medium, and Wikipedia. It analyzes digital consumption patterns using metrics such as Information Diet Score, Doomscrolling Index, sentiment analysis, learning value, misinformation risk, and filter bubble detection. The platform also provides weekly, monthly, and yearly insights to encourage mindful browsing and healthier digital habits.
